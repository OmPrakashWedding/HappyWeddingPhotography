Meet Your Photographer

Hi, I’m Om Prakash.

I’m the lead photographer behind Happy Wedding Photography. Over the years I’ve stood behind the lens at some of the most meaningful moments in people’s lives — quiet, golden-hour pre-wedding shoots, grand wedding celebrations, corporate gatherings, and the fast-moving energy of political events.

Every kind of shoot teaches me something different — patience, precision, timing. But what I chase in all of them is the same thing: the moment someone stops noticing the camera. My dream has always been simple — to capture people exactly as they are, so a photograph doesn’t just show how they looked, but how they felt.

Why Happy Wedding Photography

The moment right after “yes.”

There’s a moment I chase at every wedding — not the vows, not the posed portraits, but the instant right after, when it hits a couple that it’s real. That moment is what first drew me into wedding photography, and it’s what eventually became Happy Wedding Photography.

It’s the belief that a family’s happiness, captured honestly, becomes something they keep forever — not a performance for the camera, but proof the day was exactly as good as it felt.
