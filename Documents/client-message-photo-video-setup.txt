Here's what we need you to do:

1. Photos — Google Drive
Create this folder structure in your Google Drive (exact names matter):

HWP Website Photos/
├── Pre-Wedding/
│   └── Featured/   <- your best 3-4 shots for the homepage-style preview
├── Wedding/

- Drop all your photos for each category into that category's folder. Put your best 3-4 into the Featured subfolder too — those are the ones that show up as the small preview on the main Portfolio page.
- Naming tip: rename files so they start with numbers (01-, 02-, 03-...) — that's the order they'll appear on the site. In Windows, select all the photos, press F2, type a name, and it auto-numbers them for you.
- Quality: upload your best, full-quality edited exports — no need to shrink or compress them for the website, the site handles resizing automatically on its end. Just export as high-quality JPEG (not RAW/TIFF) so it displays correctly; full resolution is fine.
- Important — sharing: for each folder (all 8, including the Featured ones), right-click -> Share -> change "Restricted" to "Anyone with the link" -> set to Viewer. This is what lets the website display them — it doesn't make anything editable, just viewable by link.
- Once done, send us the link to each of the 4 main category folders (Pre-Wedding, Wedding, Events, Other).

2. Videos — Google Sheet
1. Create a new Google Sheet called "HWP Video Links."
2. In row 1, add these column headers exactly: category, platform, url, caption, order.
3. Add one row per video — category is one of pre-wedding, wedding, events, other; platform is youtube or instagram; url is just the normal link from YouTube's or Instagram's Share button.
4. Go to File -> Share -> Publish to web, choose CSV format, and click Publish.
5. Send us the link it gives you.
